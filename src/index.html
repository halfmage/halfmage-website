Common Questions

Everything you need to know about working with me.

What kind of projects do you work on?

I work on full redesigns, web applications, design systems, branding, and SEO/GEO optimization. From funded startups to established SMEs. If you need design, development, or both, I've probably done it.

How fast can you move?

Most projects take 1-3 months from start to launch. You'll get 2-4 updates per week, so you're always in the loop. Need something faster? Let's talk. I'm flexible.

Do you only work with big companies?

Not at all. I work with everyone from early-stage startups to established enterprises. If you're serious about building something great, I want to work with you.

What's included in the monthly price?

Design-only packages include UI/UX design, branding, and design systems. Design + Development includes everything: full-stack development, hosting setup, integrations, and ongoing support. No surprise fees.

Can you work with our existing team?

Absolutely. I integrate seamlessly with your developers, product managers, and stakeholders. Think of me as an extension of your team.

What if we need changes after launch?

I'm in it for the long haul. After launch, I can continue with iterations, improvements, and scaling. Or hand everything off cleanly. Your choice.

Do you offer guarantees?

I stand behind my work 100%. If you're not happy with the progress in the first month, I'll make it right or part ways. No hard feelings.
